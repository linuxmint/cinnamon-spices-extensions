Mint-Dock
-------------------
A native and customizable dock for Cinnamon.

#### A wide variety of options are included to ensure a good user experience: 

* Custom background color
* Rearrange dock icons and drag and drop apps from the Cinnamon menu to the dock
* Choice of the dock position displayed on the screen (top, bottom, left, right, start, center and end )
* Two hiding modes: smart hiding (Intellihide) or automatic hiding
* Several configurable options: show tooltips, show separators, show trash etc.
* The notification badge and progress bar are displayed
* Compatible with both X11 and Wayland
* And much more!
