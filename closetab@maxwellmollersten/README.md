# CloseTab - Alt+Tab close buttons

CloseTab adds close controls to Cinnamon's Alt+Tab switcher without replacing
the switcher itself.

Hold **Alt** and press **Tab** to open the switcher. You can then:

- click the `X` on a preview to close that window;
- press **Q** to close the selected window; or
- middle-click a preview to close it.

Closing uses Cinnamon's normal polite window-close request, like Alt+F4. An
application with unsaved work can still display its confirmation dialog.

## Compatibility

Close buttons are available with Cinnamon's classic `icons`, `thumbnails`, and
`preview` switcher styles, including their combinations. The coverflow and
timeline switchers do not display close buttons, but **Q** still closes the
selected window.

This release supports Cinnamon 6.6.
